# Claim-to-source ledger for `draft.md`

This ledger maps the draft's scientific claims, results, and limitations to retained records where available. Service exports and service pages are intentionally not redistributed. Their claims remain summary-only and cannot be independently checked from this tree. Internal review statements are not independently archived. Repository paths are relative to the repository root. A hash demonstrates byte identity only; it does not establish biological truth.

## Evidence-type key

- **Primary abstract:** authors' abstract, not full-paper review.
- **Complete primary patent:** inspected scan/OCR of the full patent; inventor disclosure, not peer review.
- **Retained primary-patent excerpt:** saved passage from a patent; not a claim that the complete patent or a journal article was reviewed.
- **Curated database:** structured third-party annotation; not necessarily primary text or a reviewed article.
- **Official metadata/coordinates:** deposited archive record or coordinates; metadata alone is not atom-level evidence.
- **Generated calculation:** reproducible output from retained input/code; not an experimental measurement.
- **Review synthesis:** repository analysis that points to underlying records; not an independent biological source.
